# World Design

## World
**Eidolon**

### High-Level Regions
- Human Lands
- Wilderness
- Unknown

### Initial Narrative Map
The Null above  
Asteria Kingdom ↔ Elderwood  
Old Road connects them  
Greenvale below  
Starting Forest and Forgotten Mine near Greenvale

This is a narrative map, not the final gameplay map.

## Region 01 — Greenvale / Starting Lands

### Starting Forest
Purpose:
- tutorial
- movement / jump / attack
- exploration
- first enemies

Enemies:
- Slime
- Small Wolf
- Forest Bug
- Goblin Scout

### Greenvale Village
Contains:
- Merchant
- Blacksmith
- Inn
- Quest NPC
- Guard
- Villagers
- Shrine
- Village Elder
- Safe zone

Mystery:
A locked/abandoned room appears through World Sight. A child can be seen briefly, then disappears.

### Old Road
- combat
- platforming
- hazards
- hidden paths

### Forgotten Mine
First dungeon:
- traps
- platforming
- combat
- puzzles
- treasure
- mini-boss
- lore Echo

Local story says monsters caused the mine to close. An Echo suggests villagers sealed it after discovering something.

## MVP Mini-Boss
**The Broken Miner**

Simple Phase 1 only for MVP.

Possible dialogue:
- “Don’t… open… it…”
- “Again.”

The Guardian of the Mine is reserved for a later expanded version.

## Region 02 — Elderwood
Future region:
- ancient trees
- ruins
- hidden villages
- beast territory
- magic springs
- forgotten shrine
- underground structures

## Region 03 — Asteria Kingdom
Future region:
- Capital
- Castle
- Guild
- Marketplace
- Mage Tower
- Royal District
- Slums
- Prison
- Library

Royal Library mystery:
World Sight can reveal “The History of Eidolon — Volume 0”, while normal records begin at Volume 1.

## The Null
Future forbidden region:
- not on normal map
- World Sight active
- changing terrain
- Echoes
- anomalies

It remains an in-world location, not an external fourth-wall joke.

## Region Design Rule
Each important region should contain five layers:
1. Gameplay
2. Exploration
3. NPC
4. Quest
5. Lore

Each important region requires at least:
- 1 gameplay mystery
- 1 lore mystery
