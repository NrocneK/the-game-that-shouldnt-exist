# Character Bible

## Protagonist
- Male
- Dialogue-enabled
- Temporary identity: Unknown
- True name revealed later
- Adventure / Mystery tone
- World Sight ability
- Starts in Starting Forest

## The Observer
- mysterious
- rare appearances
- calm
- few words
- avoids direct lies
- deliberately omits truth

## The Administrator
- world-management entity
- calm and logical
- not simply evil
- believes Reset preserves the world

Conflict:
**Freedom vs Stability**

## Edran
Greenvale elder.
- tutorial
- lore
- quests
- represents normal world assumptions

## Milo
Merchant.
- talkative
- practical
- money-minded
- potential memory contradictions

## Ronan
Blacksmith.
- upgrades / repairs / crafting later
- recognizes signs tied to lost forging tradition

## Lira
Future companion.
- ordinary person
- family / memories / dreams
- challenges protagonist's assumptions

## Factions
### Kingdom
Protects current order.

### Guild
Adventure, combat, exploration.

### Researchers
Magic, ancient ruins, anomalies.

### Observers
Secret group researching anomalies. Alignment unknown.

## Character Design Rule
Stick-figure characters are differentiated by:
- silhouette
- headwear
- hair / mask / helmet
- cloak / armor / robe
- backpack
- weapons
- primary / secondary / accent colors
- animation
- VFX

## Enemy Philosophy
Enemies should have identities connected to their region rather than being only HP and AI containers.

## Boss Philosophy
Every major boss should combine:
- Gameplay
- Character
- Lore
