# Art Direction

## Philosophy
**Minimal Character + Rich World**

Stick figures are the official visual identity.

## Principles
- silhouette > detail
- animation > texture complexity
- environment creates depth
- equipment differentiates characters
- VFX provide impact
- every region has visual identity

## Character Differentiation
Use:
- hats
- hair
- masks
- helmets
- hoods
- cloaks
- armor
- robes
- coats
- backpacks
- weapons
- color accents

## Animation MVP
- IDLE
- WALK
- RUN
- JUMP
- FALL
- ATTACK
- HURT
- DEATH
- DODGE

Combat animation:
Startup → Active → Recovery

## VFX
- hit spark
- slash trail
- screen shake
- knockback feedback
- enemy flash
- particles

World Sight VFX:
- subtle distortion
- outline
- afterimage
- Echo visual language

Avoid turning anomalies into comedy/glitch jokes.

## Environment Layers
1. Background
2. Far Environment
3. Gameplay Layer
4. Foreground
5. FX / UI

## MVP Art
Placeholder shapes and stick figures are acceptable.

Gameplay architecture must not depend on specific final art assets.
