# Game Concept

## Title
**The Game That Shouldn’t Exist**

## World
**Eidolon** — provisional name.

## Genre
2D side-scrolling real-time RPG Adventure.

## Tone
Adventure → Mystery → progressively darker/existential.

## Core Concept
Eidolon appears to be a normal fantasy world containing villages, cities, forests, dungeons, monsters, bosses, equipment, magic, quests, and progression.

Small inconsistencies gradually appear:
- NPCs appear where they should not.
- NPCs remember impossible events.
- Doors and locations may disappear.
- Quest information may contradict reality.
- Death and respawn eventually become narratively significant.
- The protagonist can perceive things ordinary NPCs cannot.

The story is contained entirely inside the game world. There is no external fourth-wall breaking.

## Art Philosophy
Stick figures are the official art direction, not placeholder art by necessity.

Identity comes from:
- silhouette
- weapons
- armor
- cloaks
- accessories
- animation
- effects
- environment
