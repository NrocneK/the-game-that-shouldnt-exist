# Story Bible

**Version:** 1.0

## Premise
The protagonist wakes in the Starting Forest with no memory of his origin or true name.

He can perceive things others cannot. At first this seems like an unusual supernatural ability. Over time, he discovers contradictions in Eidolon's history and reality.

The world has likely been reset at least once. The protagonist somehow was not completely reset.

An entity called **The Administrator** considers the protagonist an anomaly.

A mysterious figure called **The Observer** knows more than he reveals.

## Protagonist
- Male
- Has dialogue
- Starts as “Unknown”
- True name is revealed later
- Human-like
- Curious, practical, sometimes humorous
- Can experience fear, anger, and doubt
- Not a cold chosen-one

### Character Arc
Who am I? → Where am I? → Why can I see these things? → Why does the world contradict itself? → Who is the Administrator? → What happened before Reset? → Who was I? → What should I do with Eidolon?

The core arc is identity, not simply saving the world.

## World Sight
Temporary design/system name: **World Sight**. Technical system name: `WorldSightSystem`.

It allows the protagonist to perceive things ordinary NPCs cannot.

### Progression
1. Perception — hidden objects, paths, interactions
2. Echo — remnants or memories of the past
3. Contradiction — inconsistencies in reality/history
4. Interaction — interact with anomalies
5. Truth — connection to the protagonist's true identity

Only Stage 1 is implemented in the MVP.

## Major Characters

### The Observer
Rare, calm, speaks little, does not directly lie but omits information.

Possible secret: survived multiple Resets. This remains intentionally unconfirmed.

### The Administrator
Not a conventional demon lord.

Abilities:
- Create
- Delete
- Reset
- Modify
- Freeze
- Rollback

Philosophy:
Save World → Impossible → Reset World → Preserve World

The conflict is **Freedom vs Stability**, rather than Hero vs Evil.

### Edran
Greenvale elder. Tutorial, lore, and quest role.

### Milo
Merchant. Talkative, practical, money-minded. May remember events that never happened.

### Ronan
Blacksmith. Can recognize signs connected to a lost forging tradition.

### Lira
Potential companion introduced later. Represents ordinary people living in the current Eidolon.

## Story Outline

### Opening
- Wake in Starting Forest.
- Learn movement and combat.
- Meet an NPC who asks where the protagonist came from.
- First World Sight event: a door appears in a wall and disappears.
- Find missing merchant Milo.
- Milo says something implying this is not the first time he has been saved.
- Later, an NPC may claim to have seen the protagonist die.

### Mid Game
- More contradictions.
- Echoes reveal remnants of the past.
- Observer appears.
- The Null is discovered.
- Administrator becomes relevant.

### Late Game
- No official record of protagonist's name/history exists.
- Some NPCs remember him.
- Administrator previously reset the world.
- Administrator believes Reset is necessary to preserve Eidolon.

### Endings
Potential endings:
- Accept
- Escape
- Rewrite
- Delete
- Secret Ending

Multiple endings are outside the MVP.

## Narrative Principle
**The player should discover the truth before the protagonist does.**
